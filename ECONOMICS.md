# 📊 MyZubster Animal Registry - Economic Framework

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-29
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-29

---

## 1. Core Principle: Free Registration

**All animal registrations are FREE.**

We believe that registering animals should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial situation. This is a public good project.

---

## 2. How the Platform is Funded

The platform is sustained through:

### 2.1 Donations
- Voluntary contributions from the community
- Sent to the project wallet in Monero (XMR)
- Used to fund bounties and infrastructure

### 2.2 Premium Services (Future)
- Certificate of Registration (0.01 XMR)
- Data Analytics Reports (0.05 XMR)
- API Pro Access (0.10 XMR)

### 2.3 Sponsors & Grants
- Corporate sponsorships
- Open source grants
- Conservation funding

---

## 3. Fund Allocation

| Destination | Percentage | Description |
|-------------|------------|-------------|
| **Bounties** | **90%** | Payments to contributors |
| **Infrastructure** | **5%** | Hosting, servers, VPS |
| **Conservation** | **5%** | Animal welfare projects |
