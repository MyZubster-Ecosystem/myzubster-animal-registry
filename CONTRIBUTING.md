# 🤝 Contributing to MyZubster Animal Registry

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-29

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: Payment Rules

**All bounties are paid in Monero (XMR) on the Monero network.**

- ✅ **Accepted:** Monero (XMR) only
- ❌ **Rejected:** USDC, USDT, ETH, BTC, or any other token
- ❌ **Rejected:** PayPal, Wise, bank transfer, or any fiat

**Why Monero?**
- Micro-transactions with low fees (even €0.10 is viable)
- Full privacy - no KYC, no bank details
- Global access - anyone with internet can participate
- Decentralized - no intermediaries blocking payments

---

## Quick Start

1. **Claim an issue** with 💰 label
2. **Fork the repo** and create a branch
3. **Fix the issue** (keep it small!)
4. **Open a PR** with your **Monero (XMR) address** in the description
   - Format: `Payout Address: 4A2M4vB...`
   - ⚠️ PRs without a valid XMR address will be rejected
5. **Get paid in XMR** 🎉

---

## Rules

### For Contributors
- Only claim **one issue at a time**
- You have **24 hours** to open a PR
- PR must reference the issue: `Fixes #123`
- **MANDATORY:** Include your Monero address in PR description
- ⚠️ **USDC/USDT/ETH wallets will NOT be accepted**
- Don't share your wallet seed phrase with anyone

### For Maintainers
- Review PRs within 24 hours
- Send XMR immediately after merge
- Update FUNDING.md with each payout
- Label paid issues with `paid`

---

## Getting a Monero Wallet

1. Download: https://www.getmonero.org/downloads/
2. Create a wallet (write down the seed!)
3. Get your **public address** (starts with 4... or 8...)
4. Share ONLY this address for payments

## FAQ

**Q: Can I be paid in USDC/USDT/ETH instead?**
A: **No.** This program pays in Monero (XMR) only.

**Q: How much can I earn?**
A: Bounties range from €0.10 to €12.00 (0.06 XMR) per issue.

**Q: What if I miss the 24h deadline?**
A: The issue becomes available again. No penalties.

**Q: Are taxes involved?**
A: That depends on your country. We don't handle tax reporting.

**Q: Can I donate to the fund?**
A: Yes! Send XMR to the address in FUNDING.md.
