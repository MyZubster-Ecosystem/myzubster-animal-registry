🐾 MyZubster Web NFC Tag Simulator

Browser-side counterpart of src/nfcTag.js. Generate virtual NFC tags, simulate a scan, and test the verification flow without a physical tag or NFC reader.

1. Generate a virtual NFC tag

Fill the animal registration form. Fields marked * are required. The simulator runs entirely in this browser tab — no network calls, no external services.

2. Simulate a scan

Click Scan on a generated tag to imitate a mobile device tapping the NFC tag. The simulator decodes the URI, validates the schema, and confirms a successful round-trip.

3. Test the verification flow

Replays the registry verification: the scanner reads the tag, the page looks up the expected record, and reports whether they match.
